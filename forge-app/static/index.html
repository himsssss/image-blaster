Objects

Claude splits the scene into objects. Select one to edit it; drag on the image to redraw its box.

    Multi-angle views

    Nano Banana 2.1 via Higgsfield: a front view, then one turnaround sheet split into angles. ~5 credits per object.

    3D models

    Meshy multi-image-to-3D runs straight from this app — ~30 Meshy credits and 3–6 min per object, 8 at a time. Click a model to replace it, generate it again, upload your own or restore an older version.

    Blender

    Claude drives your open Blender through the Blender MCP: camera solve, placement, pose search, light fit, render-compare-fix loop.

    Per-object check

    Reference crop next to the same region of the latest render. Flag anything off and Claude fixes that object.

    Final project

    The finished scene: open the Blender project, grab the renders, and see what still differs from the reference.
